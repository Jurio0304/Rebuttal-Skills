# Observations from public ICLR 2027 discussions

Use these observations when choosing a response format. The study pool comprises 330 active submissions whose same-reviewer overall scores summed to a net gain of at least three before the author-discussion deadline. A second snapshot also showed a gain of at least three. The pool additionally required that the gain persisted in the November 25 snapshot. All snapshots ultimately draw on OpenReview. [Paired-score snapshot](https://github.com/papercopilot/paperlists/tree/2a8ffb27f88fe34adcd6f58b3d04007246fa4e75) · [Second snapshot](https://github.com/dipta007/iclr-2027-rebuttal-radar/tree/dc8ecd4f28c94b26e8efa779152a253f118911c6)

Public mirror pages matched all 330 forum IDs. The conservative November 5–18 visible-date window yielded 2,295 author comments across 275 papers and 424 reviewer follow-ups across 216 papers. Comments displayed only as November 19 were counted separately because the mirror lacked the hour. Counts below are papers with at least one matching phrase or HTML element. A quoted reviewer request can also trigger a match. Six cases and twelve individual tag hits were checked for meaning. An additional passage-level reading is described below. | Observable signal among 275 papers with author comments | Application to writing |
|---|---|
| 261 named a section, table, figure or other manuscript location | Give the answer in the reply and point to the exact place a reviewer can verify it. |
| 227 used numbered issues; 226 included an HTML table | Preserve the reviewer's labels. A table earns its space when the reader needs matched conditions side by side. |
| 215 used language associated with controls or ablations | Identify which explanation the control addresses. Check that the authors actually report a result before treating a requested or planned test as evidence. |
| 171 mentioned seeds or statistical variation | For a noisy comparison, supply the unit, aggregation and available uncertainty alongside the gain. |
| 169 used scope or limitation language | State a local boundary when it changes the interpretation of the result. The primary supported finding can still lead the paragraph. |

Acknowledgement language appeared in 102 of the 216 papers with reviewer follow-ups. A reviewer can accept one answer and still request another comparison. Attribute the accepted point precisely. A reply about missing access or a procedural reminder calls for a different response than a scientific objection. Selection on positive score movement makes these descriptive observations unsuitable for estimating the effect of any writing choice on scores or acceptance.

## What the sampled paragraphs actually do

For a second reading, select one author note per paper with an in-window response. Prefer a substantive note of 95–600 words, otherwise take the longest. Remove quotes and tables, take up to the first 160 words, and omit short extracts and prespecified topic matches. This produced 266 excerpts from 266 submissions. Three agents each read a disjoint set and assigned one primary communication move per excerpt. They identified 117 presenting evidence, 72 explaining an existing choice or mechanism, 35 setting the scope of a claim, 26 correcting or revising, 11 acknowledging or following up, four promising an action and one with insufficient context. These labels describe selected passages. Truncation and one coder per passage limit their precision.

Three writing decisions carry across fields:

- **Separate what was equal from what improved.** When two methods tie on the primary output but differ in time, name both observations and tie the advantage specifically to time. A favorable secondary measure deserves a precise claim.
- **Point to the comparison that changes the answer.** A reviewer asking about attribution needs to know which factor changed and which ones were held fixed. A table reference is most useful after one sentence interpreting that contrast.
- **Keep confidence proportionate to the observation.** A directional preference with intervals spanning the neutral point can motivate further study. State the observed direction and the unresolved inference in the same answer. Continue to state the findings that the comparison does establish.
