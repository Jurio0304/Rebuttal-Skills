# 7. Summarize the discussion for the area chair

Use the latest available thread records, the agreed evidence, the manuscript version and the applicable communication channel. Identify what the area chair needs to decide. When the venue has no verified author-facing global response field, the same synthesis can serve as an internal brief.

1. Open with the material changes during discussion and their implications for the central contribution.
2. Group repeated concerns by scientific question. For each, give the original concern, what the authors clarified, corrected or measured, the decisive observation and the remaining boundary. Explain when a common result addresses different reviewers in different ways.
3. Attribute reassessments to the reviewer and the dated source. Acknowledge explicit outstanding objections. Include ratings only when useful for this audience and supported by a recorded field. Describe an expressed intention using the reviewer's stated degree of commitment. Claims about consensus require support from every reviewer covered.
4. Close with the changes made or committed to and their relevant manuscript locations. A procedural request can be delivered separately through a permitted chair channel if the author seeks action.

Return a concise synthesis that stands alone for a chair who has read the paper but may have missed a thread. Keep the source notes and any unverified status for the authors. For a short field, preserve the strongest shared evidence and the unresolved issue most likely to affect the decision.
