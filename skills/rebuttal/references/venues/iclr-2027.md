# ICLR 2027 · Main conference

Checked 2026-11-25. The dates below refer to the 2026 review cycle for ICLR 2027.

| Item | Rule for authors |
| --- | --- |
| Author discussion | The author guide lists November 5–18 for public discussion. During this window authors can reply to a review multiple times. Each comment has a length limit. Check the submission's actual form for its unit and numerical cap. |
| Audience | Official reviews are publicly visible. Comments may be public or restricted through the readers selector. Select the intended readers explicitly for a private note to the AC or reviewers. |
| Revisions | Authors can update the PDF until the public discussion ends on November 18. The revised main text can run to 10 pages, up from 9 at submission. OpenReview produces a PDF diff against the original. Reviewers and ACs may disregard substantial departures and need not read every revision. |
| Later discussion | The author guide lists internal reviewer–AC discussion starting November 19. A later reviewer update provides no additional author revision window. |

For a reply citing a revision, identify the section, the change in the claim and the evidence that supports it. Verify the uploaded version before calling it available to the reviewers.
