# ICML 2026 · Research papers

Checked 2026-11-25. These instructions apply to the 2026 author–reviewer discussion.

| Item | Rule for authors |
| --- | --- |
| First round | One optional response per official review, up to 5,000 characters each, due March 30 AoE. All assigned reviewers can read each reply. A short cross-reference to another reply is permitted. |
| Follow-up | Reviewers acknowledge responses and may ask additional questions. Authors may post one final reply per reviewer who asked a follow-up, up to 5,000 characters, by April 7 AoE. Put the substantive arguments in round one. |
| Paper and supporting material | The original PDF and supplement stay fixed during discussion. Anonymous links are permitted primarily for figures, tables and descriptive captions that clarify the submitted work. Reviewers may skip links. A linked revised manuscript or rewritten passages will be disregarded. |
| AC communication | During the author–reviewer window, use the confidential AC comment for procedural questions. Extra technical rebuttals belong in the designated author response. The FAQ gives a dated exception for late reviews. |
| Visibility after decisions | Reviews and responses become public for accepted papers and for rejected papers whose authors opt in. |

For a shared concern, put the full comparison in one reply and identify it precisely in the other. Keep the finding itself visible to both readers.
